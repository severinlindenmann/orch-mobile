# orch mobile: spec and protocol gaps

Status: M0, 10 Oct 2026. Merged and deduplicated from [proposals/claude.md §5](proposals/claude.md) (5.1, 5.2, 5.3)
and [proposals/codex.md §8](proposals/codex.md). Context: [decisions.md](decisions.md).

This list is input for two readers: the owner of the orch-core spec (and the protocol), and whoever does the real
relay and host integration. The prototype runs against a FakeRelay, FakeHost and FakePrimary, so it works around
each gap with a provisional choice. Every item names where it must be fixed. "Spec" is
`orch-core/docs/architecture/orch-v2.md`; "protocol" is `orch-relay/docs/protocol-v2.md`; "issue #n" is in
orch-mobile. Section references were checked against both files on 10 Oct 2026. Items resolved by the
"Provisional answers" in decisions.md are marked "provisionally decided: D-x"; the owner may still override them.

"Must be fixed by": spec text, protocol amendment, relay (P3), host (P2), orch-mobile.

## Notes

- Items 1 to 13 are mostly text fixes. Items 14 to 25 block real relay and host integration.
- Items 5, 8, 10, 21, 26 and 29 are provisionally decided (D-3, D-5, D-4, D-1, D-2, D-6) and stay in this list
  until the owner confirms them and the spec text is changed.
- Left out on purpose: prototype-side risks that are not spec or protocol gaps (S1/S2 spikes are not production custody, FakePrimary shares the phone process, App Group and notification race limits, export compliance, App Group registration, CI Xcode pinning, app-switcher snapshots). They are handled in decisions.md.
